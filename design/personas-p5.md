## Integration and open questions / 統合と確認事項

- `lib/persona/derive.js` currently indexes only `resolution.kind === "researched"`. Consequently the eight inherited files validate but are ignored by the resolver; they can fall back to an unrelated family representative. To activate them, the owner of that file needs to index exact curated inherited IDs, preserve their recorded resolution and inherited bases, and add an offline resolver test. This task does not modify that file. / 継承ファイルは現状のリゾルバーに取り込まれません。正確な ID と継承元を保持する対応が必要です。
- The IDs `mistral-large-4-0-8`, `mistralai/mistral-medium-3-5:batch`, and `mistralai/mistral-small-4-119b-2603:thinking` were not assigned to an ancestor: exact checkpoint or mode equivalence remains unverified. A family-default result is not confirmation of equivalence. / 正確な同一性は未確認です。
- The undated `mistralai/mistral-large` and `mistralai/codestral` records have version-ambiguous provider names; their cited descriptions should be revisited if the records are pinned to different checkpoints. / 日付のない提供名が指す版は未確定です。
- Command R+ documentation gives conflicting cutoff dates in its specification and body, so its cutoff remains unknown. / Command R+ の知識の時点は公式ページ内で矛盾するため未確認としました。 [Source](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/command-r-plus).
- Empty tone, verbosity, formatting, refusal style, humour, self-description, cutoff and interface fields remain unknown where the cited evidence does not support a claim; empty lists indicate no documented claim, not an assertion of absence. / 空欄は未確認であり、性質が存在しないという意味ではありません。
