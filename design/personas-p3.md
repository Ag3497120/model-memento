# Google personas: P3 coverage and evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-16. This pass adds 30 researched and 9 inherited files (`p3-google-*.json`). It does not change pre-existing personas. Across these 39 files there are 72 non-null sourced trait entries and 281 null trait entries; inherited copies count as entries. Empty list fields are not counted as unknown traits. English values have Japanese notes. Traits describe capabilities or documented comparative behavior, not a presumed personality; publication claims are attributed to their publishers.

## Open coverage and integration issues

- `createPersonaResolver` in `lib/persona/derive.js` indexes only personas whose resolution is `researched`; it silently skips the nine inherited files. In a Google-only catalog check, `gemini-2.5-pro-ga` and `gemini-3.0-pro-preview` resolve to the unrelated 1.5 Pro family default, and `google.gemma-3-27b-it` resolves to the 2 9B family default. The reviewer should support direct inherited records, retain their ancestry and avoid fallbacks that override explicit records. This implementation file is outside P3's permitted files.
- Full catalog construction succeeded on the final check. An earlier check encountered a duplicate `llama-1` alias while other workers were editing; the collision was no longer present on the final check.
- Several IDs remain uncovered, including `gemini-3.0-flash-preview`, `gemini-2.5-flash-nothinking`, `gemini-4-argon-contributor`, various Google 3.1 thinking-level or custom-tools endpoints, 2.5 Flash-Lite June preview, Gemma base/quantized identifiers and specialist image, audio, robotics, embedding, and third-party fine-tuned models. Matching names alone do not establish identical weights, settings, task, or behavior. Verify endpoint identity individually before adding inheritance; non-chat models should not be assigned a chat persona by family alone.
- Most tone, verbosity, formatting, refusal style, humor, self-description, cutoff and model-specific failure modes remain unknown. Empty list fields likewise indicate no sourced observations, not evidence of absence. Official launch claims are not independent behavioral evaluations. Interface is left unknown where the inspected source did not establish a dialogue interface.
