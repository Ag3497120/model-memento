## Shared limits

No dated visual evidence was established for the intermediate 2026 releases. Their layouts reuse later reference evidence and are explicitly unverified, not claims that all releases looked alike. Model-switch options and defaults remain empty. Goose 0.9 documents model configuration in profiles.yaml outside the session; the exhibit does not edit that file. Recreation-only labels and the source disclosure are bilingual. Product labels stay English. Browser visual review and integration with the central registry remain outstanding.
