## Remaining work and integration limitations

- The remainder of the requested approximately 120-model audit, including full coverage of Moonshot, Zhipu, MiniMax, Qwen and the other vendors, is **not done**. Every field not explicitly checked in the table remains **unverified**; the presence of an HTTPS URL is not proof of its associated claims. Existing summaries, dates, prices, knowledge cutoffs, successor claims and status values elsewhere in the catalogs require review.
- No official confirmation was found for the pre-existing `grok-2.5` override; it was removed rather than giving it a source that does not substantiate it. The underlying base record and its inferred specs/status can still be exposed and are **unverified**. Correcting or removing the base record requires changes outside this task's files or an explicit supported override for clearing values.
- The build now supplies `fact_sources` for corrected fields, carries closed override sources and translations, applies overrides to the fallback, and filters the denylisted domain. Existing evidence without field associations is retained for other facts; its relevance is **unverified**. Some answer paths in `web/js/closure.js` still use a flat list of all model sources rather than `fact_sources`, so those paths can cite unrelated retained evidence. Generated `web/data/*.json` is not updated here; the build was not run in this task.
- `gpt-4o-2024-05-13` still has 8,192 context in the curated base. The current GPT-4o page describes the alias and lists the older snapshot but does not explicitly assign its 128,000 window to that snapshot; exact snapshot value remains **unverified** in this pass.
- The other pre-existing `new_curated_models` entries and `closed_overrides` have not been exhaustively checked. The official source links added to existing closed overrides identify relevant provider documentation, but do not certify every historical claim in their summaries or their knowledge cutoffs. Some model-page search snippets may be stale; check the current page and provider-specific service before changing lifecycle state.
- The offline tests check HTTPS syntax, calendar-valid dates, and numeric constraints; they do not fetch pages, prove source relevance, identify all stale inherited values, or test the full build.
