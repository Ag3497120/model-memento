## Integration and remaining gaps

- **Resolver change needed outside this assignment:** `createPersonaResolver` in `lib/persona/derive.js` indexes only `resolution.kind === "researched"`, ignoring these curated inherited records. In the local resolver check, `grok-4-0709` fell back to `xai:grok-2` as a family-default and the other two variants returned unknown. The reviewer should make curated inherited records directly resolvable without relabeling inherited evidence as researched, test alias collisions and registry boundaries, and suppress inappropriate family fallback.
- The resolver also assumes every ID containing a slash belongs to the Hugging Face registry, although several supplied closed/API IDs contain slashes. Review registry matching for these records; schema validity alone does not establish that every API entry will resolve.
- Coverage is partial. Grok 2.5, Kimi K2.8, and other distinct later models and the numerous speed, free, hosted, quantized, safety-modified and task-specific variants remain unresearched unless listed above. Their identity or behavior has not been established merely by their names. Kimi K1.5 and a plain GLM-4 ID did not appear in the supplied records; the listed GLM-4 32B record is the identified April variant. MiniMax-01 is a series containing both Text-01 and VL-01 ([official source](https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-01-series-2)); the ambiguous `minimax/minimax-01` ID was not equated with Text-01. MiniMax-M1 has separately trained 40k and 80k versions ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13585)); the specific `minimaxai/minimax-m1-80k` record was not equated with an unspecified M1 deployment.
- Tone, verbosity, formatting, humor, most refusal behavior, most self-descriptions, most failure modes and all knowledge cutoffs remain unknown where the actual model-specific behavior was not verified. Provider capability claims are not proof of a distinctive personality or of performance in every setting. Interface is left unknown unless the cited evidence identifies the model's conversational format.
