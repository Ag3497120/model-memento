The 2023 [landing component](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Main/Landing.jsx) contains the historical heading and three columns; [TextChat](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Main/TextChat.jsx) and [ModelMenu](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Models/ModelMenu.jsx) place the selector at the input. The [rail](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Nav/index.jsx) specifies 260px desktop width, dark background and conversations; [NewChat](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Nav/NewChat.jsx), [ClearConvos](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Nav/ClearConvos.jsx), [DarkMode](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Nav/DarkMode.jsx) and [NavLinks](https://github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat/blob/5af03bb3d8fb8556403d59f847e147dac5d4019e/client/src/components/Nav/NavLinks.jsx) supply the control labels. Limits: exact deployments, theme preference, exact rendered dimensions outside the quoted CSS, post-March 2026 sidebar width and all model choices/defaults are unverified. The greeting depends on time and configuration; "Good morning" is one sourced example. Later navigation buttons can be permission-dependent and some are icon-only upstream; their text labels are exposed here in lieu of reproducing icons. The sidebar and early light colors outside the cited values, later token resolution after the stylesheet migration, and chat-history contents are approximations. The product can vary its landing form position by setting. The 2023 screen includes only one short example per column. No screenshots, product icons or logos are reproduced. No inference is made that a release candidate visibly changed when the checked code did not demonstrate it.
